Security Tips and Recommendations

Based on your risk assessment results, here are steps you can take to protect your business:

Always use strong passwords

Use long, unique passwords for each account, ones that can't easily be guessed.

Use multi-factor authentication

Mandate a second step at login for personal and business accounts.

Keep software updated

Install updates for operating systems and apps to maintain security.

Back up your data

Keep regular backups of important files, stored separately from your main computers.

Train your employees

Quarterly cyber awareness training for employees, to prevent phishing and other cyber threats.

Secure your Wi-Fi

Use a strong Wi-Fi password and host guest on a seperate Wi-Fi server.
